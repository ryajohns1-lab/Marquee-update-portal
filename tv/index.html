How do I install on Fire TV? +

Use Downloader on your Fire TV to open this website, enter your authorized access code, and install the signed TV APK. Existing installations can use Settings → Check for Updates. For private account and saved settings, use your Ovrelixa profile inside the app.

Downloader short codes will be shown only after they have been registered and tested.

Ovrelixa TV is a player, not a content provider. It does not supply subscriptions, channels, movies, TV shows, guide services, or playlists. Use only sources you are authorized to access. Smart EPG recognition is optional; station artwork and playback options depend on source availability.
