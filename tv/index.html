How do I install on Fire TV? +

Open Downloader on your Fire TV, visit this website or the verified download address, and install the APK. The transition build does not force activation. To enroll for future secure updates, use the TV profile settings inside Ovrelixa TV.

Downloader short codes will be shown only after they have been registered and tested.

Ovrelixa TV is a player, not a content provider. It does not include TV subscriptions, playlists, channels, movies, or series. Add content from providers you are authorized to use.
